# User conversations

A log of conversations with people who build on Portaldot (or would), and what each one changed in PalletDock. Add a row after every conversation. Only real conversations go here: no composite, hypothetical, or paraphrased-from-memory entries.

## How to log one

- **Date**: the day of the conversation (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Who**: role and context, e.g. "Solidity developer building on Portaldot V3 testnet" or "Portaldot community moderator". Use a name or handle only with the person's permission.
- **Channel**: Discord, call, chat, in person, issue.
- **What they said**: their words, quoted or closely paraphrased, including what they did not need or did not like.
- **What changed**: the concrete change it led to (commit, issue, doc page), or "no change" with the reason.

Do not record private keys, seed phrases, wallet balances, or contact details here.
